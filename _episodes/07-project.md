# 1. How to participate

As a participant of this workshop (US-based or international, in-person or remote, UB or non-UB), 
you are eligible for the mini-project competition with the monetary prizes. We will have the following prize categories:

* 1st prize - 1 student $300

* 2nd prize - 3 students, $200 each

* 3rd prize - 5 students, $100 each
 

The process is the following:

## Step 1. At workshop 

Save and document your work during the workshop, initiate your projects.

## Step 2. A week after

Work on your project during the week following the end of the workshop. You will still have access to UB CCR resources and software, but you
may want to start using your home institution resources.


## Step 3. Submit your materials via GitHub

To participate in the competition, please ensure your representative coursework and the course projects are accepted to 
 [this GitHub repository](https://github.com/compchem-cybertraining/Cyber_Training_Workshop2026_course_projects) by July 18 (Saturday), 5 pm Buffalo time. 


## Step 4. Participate in the oral competition

The committee of instrcutors will score the written projects by July 22th and a sub-set of participants will be selected to present their works orally (via Zoom) on 
July 24-26 (the exact dates are to be determined). The presentations will be videorecorded and shared on the CyberTraining website (down below).
