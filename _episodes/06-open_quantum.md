## 1. Plan of the Day

**Morning, 9 am - noon**

Theory of quantum dynamics in open systems. Lecture (90 min)
Obtaining and installing TENSO (https://ifgroup.github.io/pytenso/)
Example 1: Thermal relaxation of a two-surface molecule
Example 2: Computing with Tensor Trees and Tensor Trains
Example 3: Competing Thermal Environments
Example 4: Laser-Driven Molecule Immersed in Solvent

**Noon - 1:30 pm Lunch break**

**Afternoon, 1:30 pm - 5:00 pm**
Example 5: Time Propagation Strategies
Example 6: Fenna-Matthews-Olson complex
Testing Convergence
Changing Units
Troubleshooting
Capstone Project: Superexchange in the presence of a thermal environment
Capstone Project: Entanglement Sudden Death

**Evening 6:30 pm Optional trip to Niagara Falls**
